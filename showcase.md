# Banking dashboard that enables banking employees to make informed decisions on friction points.

_Product School · Vibe Coding Certification · Final Project Showcase_

**Live product:** https://the-dashboard-nobody-reads-3.replit.app/

## The Problem & Hypothesis

**Problem.** A plain-language landing dashboard covering banking apps and tools, with useful filters, visible friction points, and recommended actions, will help bank employees find and use the information rather than abandon the dashboard or rely on unverified alternatives.

Expected evidence is lower employee-dashboard bounce, increased repeat use and drill-down, and evidence that teams explore friction and recommendations during product iterations.

**Hypothesis.** We believe a first screen that has a landing dashboard with all the apps and tools used by banking clients, in plain-language, with the ability to filter , and shows the high level friction points with the recommended action/s will cause users to find the dashboard useful and use the data shown instead of using other unverified data sources or not using it all for banking employees who make critical decisions on the direction of apps/tools used by banking customers. We'll know we're right when bounce rate drops, there is an increase in the use of the dashboard, including drilling down into detail, friction points and recommended actions are explored by the team as part of the iterations done to the tools and apps.

## Data Signal → The One Fix

| Bring the priority friction and recommended next action higher on Overview | The current placement makes the most decision-relevant content easy to miss. Show a concise priority signal and action near the top, with a clear route to the Frictions detail. | visible friction, and recommended actions, will help bank employees find and use relevant information rather than abandon the dashboard or rely on unverified alternatives. |
